Resku

Resku resolves communication and spatial blind spots in structural fires and collapsed buildings. By combining high-density LiDAR SLAM with localized thermal fusion and biometric monitoring, Resku maintains operational awareness in zero-visibility zones.

Thermal Sensor Fusion

Edge-processed computer vision separates human heat signatures through dense smoke and structural debris.

Responder Telemetry

Tracks live core vitals and environmental exposure thresholds to alert incident commanders prior to exhaustion.
